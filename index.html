Demoscene Tribute

WebGL Experiments Inspired by the Amiga Era

Welcome to a collection of real-time graphics demos paying homage to the legendary demoscene of the 1980s and 1990s. These experiments recreate classic effects using modern WebGL2 and Web Audio APIs.

The demoscene was an underground computer art subculture that pushed hardware to its absolute limits, creating stunning audiovisual experiences on machines like the Commodore Amiga, Atari ST, and C64.

Chromium Dreams

A multi-part demo featuring 8 classic effects: copper bars, plasma, starfield, tunnel, metaballs, vector balls, fractal zoom, and raymarched infinite forms. Includes procedural chiptune music and sine-wave scroller.
